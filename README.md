Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard built for Formation BD, a sports-merchandise e-commerce brand in Bangladesh. The goal of the dashboard is to provide clear visibility into sales performance, customer behavior, and product-level insights—enabling the team to make faster and more informed business decisions.

The dashboard aggregates sales data from multiple product lines and transforms it into meaningful KPIs, visualizations, and geographic insights.

1. Sales KPIs

Total Number of Orders

Total Sales (Sum of Sale)

High-level metrics updated dynamically based on slicer selections.

2. Product-Level Breakdown

Pie chart showing distribution of sales among different product categories:

Flags

Bottles

Tshirts

Decal

PhoneCase

Posters

Helps identify top-performing and low-performing items.

3. Sales Trend Over Time

Line chart visualizing daily sales volume.

Makes it easy to spot:

Sales spikes

Seasonal patterns

Days with unusually low activity

Particularly useful for monitoring the impact of marketing campaigns and match-day events.

4. Orders by Customer Location

Interactive map of Bangladesh marking order density by region.

Helps Formation BD understand:

Customer geographic distribution

Regional demand

Opportunities for targeted marketing or offline activation

5. Interactive Slicers

Date filters providing the ability to analyze specific periods.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop

Power Query for cleaning and shaping the raw dataset

DAX for KPI calculations

Bing Maps Integration for location visualization

📁 Project Workflow

Data Import – Loaded raw order dataset exported from Formation BD’s e-commerce backend.

Data Cleaning – Removed inconsistencies, formatted dates, standardized product labels.

Data Modeling – Established relationships and built a fact table for sales.

Visualization – Built multiple charts, KPIs, and maps using Power BI visuals.

Insights & Decision Support – Used the dashboard to identify:

Best-selling product categories

Regional hotspots

Sales peaks (e.g., match-day boosts)

Future inventory & marketing needs

📈 Business Impact

This dashboard directly contributed to Formation BD’s ability to make data-driven decisions, such as:

Adjusting inventory based on popular products

Focusing advertising on regions with high customer density

Planning campaigns around peak sales dates

Monitoring overall business health in real time

It has become a valuable internal reporting tool for understanding customer behavior and optimizing operations.
